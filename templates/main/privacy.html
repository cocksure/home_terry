This policy explains what personal data HOME TERRY TEXTILE LLC collects through the website hometerry.uz, why we collect it and how you can control it. We collect only what we need to answer your request.

1. Who we are

The operator of your personal data is HOME TERRY TEXTILE LLC (OOO "HOME TERRY TEXTILE"), 7 Buyuk Ipak Yuli St., Samarkand, Republic of Uzbekistan. Contact for any privacy question: info@hometerry.uz.

2. What data we collect

When you send the contact form: your name, phone number, email, company name (optional), subject, message text and the type of request you chose.

Technical data: your IP address. We use it to protect the form from spam (no more than 3 requests per minute from one address) and it is included in the request so we can detect abuse. Like any website, our server also keeps standard access logs.

We do not ask for passport data, payment card details, biometric or any other sensitive data. Please do not include such data in your message.

3. Why we use it

  • to answer your question and send you a price list or a commercial offer;
  • to discuss and fulfil an order or cooperation you asked about;
  • to protect the website from spam and abuse.

We do not send advertising mailings and do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

4. Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent, which you give by ticking the box in the contact form, in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan "On Personal Data" No. ZRU-547 of 2 July 2019. For visitors from the European Union, the basis is your consent and steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR).

5. Where your request goes

Requests from the form are delivered to the company's internal work chat in the Telegram messenger, which is operated by Telegram (servers outside Uzbekistan). By sending the form you agree to this transfer. Only employees who handle client requests have access to this chat.

We may disclose data to state authorities only when required by law.

6. How long we keep it

We keep requests for as long as needed to answer them and to continue the cooperation you asked about, but no longer than 3 years after your last contact with us. After that, the requests are deleted.

7. Cookies and browser storage

  • csrftoken — a technical cookie that protects forms from forgery;
  • django_language — remembers the language you chose;
  • theme, fontSize — stored only in your browser (localStorage) to remember the dark/light theme and text size.

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. If this changes, we will update this policy.

8. Your rights

You can at any time ask us to tell you what data we hold about you, correct it, delete it or stop processing it, and you can withdraw your consent. Write to info@hometerry.uz — we will reply within 30 days. Visitors from the EU may also lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.

9. Security

The website works over an encrypted HTTPS connection, the form is protected from spam and forgery, and access to requests is limited to the employees who need it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add new services. The current version is always available on this page; the date of the last update is shown at the top.
